## The Problem: Schedule Gridlock
I saw students stuck with bad schedules. Manual swapping in group chats was slow and error-prone.

## The Solution: Seamless Auto-Matching
I built **KRSwitch** as smart trading floor. Students list owned class and desired class.

I engineered platform to handle negotiations instantly. When I detect mutual match, system locks swap automatically. Both parties get ideal timetable. Zero friction.

## My Impact
- **Zero Friction:** I eliminated manual negotiations.
- **Fairness Guarantee:** I implemented atomic swaps. No double-booking.
- **Administrative Relief:** I built operator dashboard for staff. They monitor telemetry and execute overrides.

---

## My Design Choices
I chose utilitarian UI focus. For light mode, I used Microsoft Excel aesthetic. Prioritizes data density. For dark mode, I used Stockbit aesthetic. Sleek high-contrast environment for real-time monitoring.

## How I Built It
I designed engine for highly sensitive concurrent transactions.
- **Real-Time Bidding:** I integrated Socket.IO. Schedule availability changes instantly.
- **Atomic Matchmaking:** I wrapped swaps in PostgreSQL transactions. Prevents overlapping classes.
- **Fluid Interface:** I optimized React frontend with state batching and linear interpolation. Maintains 60fps under load.
- **Database Modeling:** I used Prisma ORM. Simplifies complex relationships into readable schemas.
