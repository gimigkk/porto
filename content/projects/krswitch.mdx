## The Problem

University students often get stuck with inconvenient class schedules that clash with their preferences — different sections, bad time slots, or unwanted lecturers.

## The Solution

KRSwitch automatically matches students who want to swap class sections. The platform handles the entire negotiation process in real-time, ensuring both parties get the timetable they actually want.

## Key Features

- **Real-time auto-matching** — the system finds compatible swap partners the moment they become available
- **Barter offer system** — students can propose, accept, or decline swaps with full transparency
- **Email notifications** — automatic alerts when matches are found or schedules change
- **Admin dashboard** — complete oversight of all swap activities with management tools
