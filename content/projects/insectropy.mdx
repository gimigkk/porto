### 1. 3D Physics Dice Simulation in a 2D Viewport

One of our standout technical contributions was designing and programming the hybrid **3D Physics-Driven D20 Combat Resolver**:

- **Real-Time 3D Physics in 2D**: Rather than settling for pseudorandom UI counters, we embedded a real-time Godot 3D physics sub-viewport (`Dice3DPhysicsSandbox.gd` & `CombatDice3D.gd`) directly on top of the 2D isometric combat plane.
- **Physical Dice Throw**: Dice are spawned with randomized torque and impulse vectors, colliding with invisible collision boundaries before resting. An orientation matrix checker reads the upward-facing face normal to determine the actual roll outcome (1-20), feeding back into the turn-based action calculator.
- **D&D Combat Math & Modifiers**: Attack actions query target Armor Class (AC), player dexterity, and luck modifiers (`D20 + floor(LCK / 5)`). Critical rolls (natural 20) trigger dramatic screen-shake, chromatic aberration, and bonus damage multipliers.

### 2. Tactical Grid Engine & Hazard Mechanics

Tactical combat requires pixel-perfect predictability. We engineered a robust isometric tile interaction matrix:

- **State-Driven Turn Core**: Built around an asynchronous queue (`TurnManager.gd` & `CombatActionResolver.gd`) that prevents race conditions, soft-locks, and animation desyncs when both players queue simultaneous abilities.
- **Environmental Hazards & Elemental Reactions**:
  - **Fire Spread & Heat Distortion**: Implemented interactive fire tiles with custom GLSL shaders featuring screen-space heat refraction. Flammable wooden crates and foliage catch fire dynamically.
  - **Ice Momentum & Sliding**: Units pushed or stepped onto frozen tiles slide continuously across unobstructed lines via `ForcedMovementResolver.gd`.
  - **Water Drowning**: Knocking enemies or players into deep water tiles triggers an immediate drowning state machine, sinking their sprites below the waterline with custom vertex shaders.
- **Dynamic Telegraphing & Occlusion**: Custom tile shaders render multi-tier threat borders (orange for patrol pathways, crimson red for pending lethals) while providing 50% alpha ghosting when tall environmental props occlude heroes.

### 3. Modular Enemy AI & Boss Behavior Trees

Combat depth hinges on reactive adversary brains:

- **Modular AI Architecture**: Decoupled AI profiles inheriting from `AIBrain.gd`, including `SimpleMeleeBrain`, `SimpleRangedBrain`, `DungslayerBrain`, and the multi-phase `BossButterflyBrain`.
- **Heuristic Pathfinding**: Rather than raw Dijkstra spam, enemies calculate proximity weights, hazard avoidance, and flanking angles. If a primary path is blocked by ice or fire, the AI chooses tactical secondary staging tiles.
- **Airborne Boss Phases**: The butterfly boss hovers above ground-level hazard tiles, ignoring floor traps while commanding directional wind shields and aerial gale sweeps.

### 4. Custom Shaders & Graphics Optimization

- **Custom GLSL Shaders**: Authored custom shaders for dithered flame effects, atmospheric god rays (`Juicy Fruit Upon The Hill`), procedural celestial halos on the escape boat, and backdrop Gaussian blurs for UI modals.
- **VRAM & Performance Optimization**: Enforced texture compression pipelines and culled inactive card viewports, ensuring buttery 60 FPS performance on low-spec integrated GPUs as well as desktop graphics cards.

### 1. Simulasi Dadu Fisika 3D di Dalam Viewport 2D

Salah satu inovasi teknis paling menonjol yang kami rancang dan implementasikan adalah sistem **Combat Resolver Dadu D20 Berbasis Fisika 3D Nyata**:

- **Fisika 3D Real-Time di Dunia 2D**: Daripada memakai hitungan RNG angka acak UI biasa, kami mengintegrasikan sub-viewport fisika 3D Godot langsung (`Dice3DPhysicsSandbox.gd` & `CombatDice3D.gd`) di atas layar pertarungan isometrik 2D.
- **Lemparan Dadu Fisik Realistis**: Dadu dilempar dengan impuls dan torsi putar acak, memantul di dinding collider tak terlihat sebelum mendarat. Matrix orientasi menghitung vektor normal sisi atas dadu untuk menentukan angka hasil lemparan (1-20).
- **Matematika D&D & Critical Hit**: Angka dadu dihitung bersama Armor Class (AC) musuh, stat Dexterity pemain, serta formula keberuntungan (`D20 + floor(LCK / 5)`). Critical roll (angka 20 alami) langsung memicu screen-shake dramatis, chromatic aberration, dan damage ekstra.

### 2. Engine Grid Taktikal & Sistem Bahaya Lingkungan

Game taktikal butuh kepastian mekanisme pergerakan yang presisi. Kami membangun matriks interaksi ubin (*tile*) yang sangat solid:

- **Asynchronous Turn Queue**: Menggunakan antrean state-driven (`TurnManager.gd` & `CombatActionResolver.gd`) guna mencegah konflik input, soft-lock, atau tabrakan giliran saat kedua pemain memilih ability di waktu bersamaan.
- **Bahaya Lingkungan & Reaksi Elemen**:
  - **Penyebaran Api & Pembiasan Panas**: Ubin api interaktif memakai custom shader GLSL dengan efek pembiasan gelombang panas (screen-space heat refraction). Objek kayu dan semak dapat terbakar secara dinamis.
  - **Momentum Meluncur di Es**: Unit yang didorong ke ubin beku akan meluncur lurus tanpa henti hingga menabrak rintangan lewat `ForcedMovementResolver.gd`.
  - **Mekanik Tenggelam (Drowning)**: Musuh atau pemain yang terpental ke air dalam akan langsung masuk ke state tenggelam dan tenggelam perlahan ke bawah ubin air.
- **Telegraphing & Occlusion Ghosting**: Shader ubin menampilkan kotak peringatan bahaya (oranye untuk jalur musuh, merah untuk area serangan mematikan) dan memberikan transparansi 50% otomatis saat objek lingkungan menutupi karakter pemain.

### 3. AI Musuh Modular & Perilaku Boss

- **Arsitektur AI Berbasis Profil**: Memisahkan profil AI secara modular mewarisi `AIBrain.gd`, termasuk `SimpleMeleeBrain`, `SimpleRangedBrain`, `DungslayerBrain`, dan `BossButterflyBrain` multi-fase.
- **Pathfinding Cerdas**: Musuh tidak sekadar mengejar garis lurus; mereka memperhitungkan bobot ubin bahaya dan taktik mengepung. Jika jalur terhalang api atau duri, AI mencari rute alternatif yang paling optimal.
- **Mekanik Boss Melayang**: Boss kupu-kupu memiliki status melayang (airborne) sehingga kebal terhadap jebakan ubin lantai sembari melepaskan badai angin dan tameng proyektil.

### 4. Custom Shader & Optimasi Rendering

- **Shader GLSL Khusus**: Membuat shader api ber-dither, god rays atmosferik pada event naratif (`Juicy Fruit Upon The Hill`), lingkaran cahaya suci pada perahu penyelamat, dan background blur Gaussian di modal UI.
- **Optimasi Performa**: Menggunakan kompresi tekstur VRAM efisien serta culling viewport kartu non-aktif, menjaga game berjalan mulus di 60 FPS bahkan pada laptop dengan grafis terintegrasi (iGPU).
