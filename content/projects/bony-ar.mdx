## Immersive Anatomy Learning

*Bony-AR* is an interactive educational tool designed to help anatomy students memorize the human skeletal system. Built with Unity and AR Foundation, the app leverages mobile augmented reality to project a true-to-scale, medically accurate 3D skeleton directly into the user's physical environment.

As **Lead Programmer and Sole 3D Artist**, I built the entire pipeline: modeled and optimized anatomy in Blender, then engineered the AR rendering and interaction systems in Unity.

---

### 1. The Anatomy Quiz Engine
The crown jewel of the application is the **Interactive Quiz Minigame**, which transforms passive learning into a high-stakes, gamified experience.

- **Dynamic State Machine**: Built `BoneQuizController` to manage game flow, handling UI transitions and timer countdowns.
- **Escalating Difficulty**: Engineered a dynamic timing algorithm that actively shrinks the time limit as players successfully complete loops, forcing rapid recall under pressure.
- **Haptic & Audio Feedback**: Integrated native Android vibration APIs and custom audio cues to provide immediate, satisfying feedback upon correct or incorrect bone selections, complete with a visual "heart loss" animation system.

### 2. High-Performance AR Rendering
Rendering 206 individual, interactable bones in real-time AR environments requires extreme optimization to prevent mobile devices from overheating or dropping frames.

- **GPU Instancing**: Restructured the rendering pipeline to draw all 206 bones in a single draw call, ensuring a locked 60 FPS experience even on older devices.
- **LayerMask Isolation**: Heavily optimized physics raycasting by isolating interactive bones onto dedicated physics layers, ensuring that screen-taps resolve instantly without unnecessary computational overhead.

### 3. Comprehensive 3D Asset Pipeline
To ensure the app looked as good as it ran, I owned the complete 3D asset pipeline.

- **Blender Preparation**: Meticulously separated, named, and optimized a dense human skeleton model (`tulang bulang wowok.blend`), ensuring accurate pivots and appropriate polycounts for mobile AR constraints.
- **Data-Driven Architecture**: Architected a `BoneInfoDatabase` using Unity ScriptableObjects to map every single 3D mesh to its correct Indonesian medical terminology, creating a clean separation between art assets and gameplay logic.
