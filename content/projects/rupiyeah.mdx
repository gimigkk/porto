Yes — another developer made another budgeting app. But every good dev eventually solves a real problem, and nine times out of ten, that problem is their own lack of money. Rupiyeah is that app.

## Overview

Rupiyeah is a fully offline personal budgeting app built with Flutter. All your financial data lives entirely on your device — no accounts, no cloud sync, no internet required. It targets the growing need for private, fast, and reliable money management across every major platform.

## The Problem

Most budgeting apps require accounts, network connections, and trust in a third-party with your financial data. Rupiyeah takes the opposite stance: **your data is yours, period**. Every transaction, every tag, every report stays locally on your device via Hive, a blazing-fast NoSQL database built for Flutter.

## Key Features

- **Smart Daily Budgeting** — set a monthly budget and let Rupiyeah automatically calculate your safe daily spend limit, or override it manually
- **Transaction Management** — add, edit, and categorize transactions with fully custom tags
- **Document Attachments** — attach receipts or photos directly to transactions
- **Multiple Payment Methods** — track cash wallet and bank accounts separately
- **Export Reports** — generate PDF reports with embedded charts, or export raw CSV for spreadsheet analysis
- **Home Screen Widgets** — glanceable daily budget and quick-add transaction shortcut without opening the app
- **6 Themes** — Purple, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Rose, and Midnight — all with dark mode support

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter** — cross-platform UI framework targeting Android, iOS, Web, Windows, macOS, and Linux from a single codebase
- **Hive** — lightweight, fast key-value store with no native dependencies
- **fl_chart** — beautiful, animated charts for spending visualization
- **Syncfusion PDF** — rich PDF generation with charts embedded inline
- **home_widget** — native home screen widget bridge for Android and iOS

## Privacy Model

Rupiyeah has a zero-trust external model by design:

- No internet permission required
- No analytics or telemetry
- No third-party SDKs that phone home
- All data lives in the app's sandboxed local storage
