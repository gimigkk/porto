Another developer, another budgeting app. But every dev eventually solves their own problem. For me, that problem was tracking money without cloud sync.

## Overview

Rupiyeah is a fully offline personal budgeting app built with Flutter. All financial data lives entirely on your device. No accounts. No cloud sync. No internet.

## The Problem

Most budgeting apps require accounts, network connections, and trust in a third-party with your financial data. Rupiyeah takes the opposite stance: **your data is yours, period**. Every transaction, every tag, every report stays locally on your device via Hive, a blazing-fast NoSQL database built for Flutter.

## Key Features

- **Smart Daily Budgeting**: Set a monthly budget and let Rupiyeah calculate your safe daily spend limit, or override manually.
- **Transaction Management**: Add, edit, and categorize transactions with custom tags.
- **Document Attachments**: Attach receipts or photos directly to transactions.
- **Multiple Payment Methods**: Track cash wallet and bank accounts separately.
- **Export Reports**: Generate PDF reports with embedded charts or raw CSVs.
- **Home Screen Widgets**: Glanceable daily budget and quick-add shortcuts.
- **6 Themes**: Purple, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Rose, and Midnight.

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter**: Cross-platform UI framework.
- **Hive**: Lightweight, fast key-value store.
- **fl_chart**: Animated charts for spending visualization.
- **Syncfusion PDF**: Rich PDF generation with inline charts.
- **home_widget**: Native home screen widget bridge.

## Privacy Model

Rupiyeah has a zero-trust external model by design:

- No internet permission required
- No analytics or telemetry
- No third-party SDKs that phone home
- All data lives in the app's sandboxed local storage
